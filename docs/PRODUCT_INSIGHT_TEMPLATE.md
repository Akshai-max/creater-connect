# Product and market insight: working template (workbook tab 01_INSIGHT)

**Worth 10 points.** "Use the product access in your invitation email. Facts need evidence; ideas must be clearly labelled as ideas."

Only you can fill this in, because it has to come from your own session with IncuBrix. Plan on about 60–90 minutes, and screen-record the whole session: the recording is your evidence, and it can be reused for the product walkthrough video.

## Before you start
- [ ] Start a screen recording (OBS, Loom, or Windows Game Bar with Win+Alt+R)
- [ ] Have a real piece of content ready to run through the product, such as a 20–60 minute video or podcast episode you're allowed to use
- [ ] Open a notes file and timestamp everything: `[mm:ss] what I did → what happened`

## Session plan (act as the target creator)

| # | Product area to try | What to note | Evidence to capture |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sign-up and onboarding | Steps to the first useful result, confusing screens, time taken | Screenshot of each step, plus total minutes |
| 2 | Upload or import content | Supported sources, size limits, failures | Screenshot and any error text |
| 3 | Core output (clips, captions, repurposing, editing, publishing, whichever the product does) | Quality, accuracy, how much fixing it needed | Before and after screenshots, plus a sample output file |
| 4 | Publishing and scheduling or export | Which platforms, and what's missing | Screenshot |
| 5 | Collaboration and team workflow | Invites, roles, review flow | Screenshot |
| 6 | Pricing and upgrade prompts | Where they appear and whether they're clear | Screenshot |

If a feature is unavailable or broken, record the **date, time and screenshot**. The brief says not to work around restrictions.

## A. Five observations (fill one row per workbook line)

For each observation, record: the product area; what happened (a fact, with a timestamp); what worked or was difficult; the evidence (screenshot or recording timestamp, or a file link); and why it matters for growth.

Template for each one:
> **Area:** …
> **What happened (fact):** At [mm:ss] I … and the product …
> **Worked / difficult:** …
> **Evidence:** `insight/obs1.png`, recording 04:12
> **Why it matters for growth:** Creators in our lead list say they need *[need]* (see the need column in leads.csv). This step makes that [easier/harder] because …

Tips:
- Be specific, with numbers: "took 7 min to first clip", "captions had 3 errors per minute on accented speech".
- Tie each observation to a need column in your leads (editing, repurposing, captions, consistency, backlog, team_workflow).
